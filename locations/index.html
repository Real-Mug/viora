Service areas

Where we work

We take on properties only in markets where we have reliable cleaning and maintenance capacity. That is a shorter list than 'across Canada', and it is a more useful one.

Ontario

  • Toronto

    Short-term rental co-hosting and management for condo and house owners across Toronto, in a market shaped by principal-residence rules and building bylaws.

    Downtown and the Financial District · Harbourfront and CityPlace · King West and Liberty Village and more

    Local guide

  • Muskoka

    Vacation rental management for cottage owners across Muskoka, where the season is short, suppliers are stretched and township rules differ.

    Township of Muskoka Lakes · Bracebridge · Gravenhurst and more

    Local guide

British Columbia

  • Vancouver

    Co-hosting and rental management for Vancouver owners, in a market where provincial and city rules tightly define what can be let short-term.

    Downtown and Coal Harbour · West End · Yaletown and more

    Local guide

  • Whistler

    Management for Whistler owners, in a zoning-defined market with two strong seasons and demanding guest expectations.

    Whistler Village and Village North · Upper Village and Blackcomb Benchlands · Creekside and more

    Local guide

Quebec

  • Montreal

    Co-hosting and management for Montreal owners, in a market with provincial registration requirements and strong festival-driven seasonality.

    Le Plateau-Mont-Royal · Ville-Marie and downtown · Mile End and more

    Local guide

Alberta

  • Calgary

    Co-hosting and rental management for Calgary owners, in a licensed market with strong event and corporate demand.

    Beltline and downtown · Kensington and Hillhurst · Inglewood and Ramsay and more

    Local guide

Property outside these areas?

Send it to us anyway. Whether we can take on a new market depends on whether we can find cleaning and maintenance capacity we trust there - sometimes we can, sometimes we cannot, and we will tell you which it is rather than take the property and hope.
