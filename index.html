Why owners work with us

Built for property owners. Designed around guests.

Short-term rentals succeed on the boring things done consistently: a clean property, a quick reply, an accurate listing and an owner who knows what is happening. That is the work we take on.

  • Professional management

    Defined standards for how your property is cleaned, presented and maintained, with the work checked rather than assumed.

  • Responsive guest support

    Guests reach a real person across the stay, with response standards agreed with you in writing rather than implied.

  • Listing optimization

    Content, structure and positioning built around the guest making the decision, kept current as the property changes.

  • Reliable operations

    Turnovers, maintenance and suppliers coordinated against the live calendar, including the last-minute changes.

  • Transparent communication

    Regular written reporting on what happened at the property, what it cost, and what we think is worth changing.

  • Technology-supported

    Systems for scheduling, messaging and reporting, used to remove repetitive work rather than to replace judgement.

How it works

How working with VioraRental starts

Four steps, no obligation until the scope and the fee are agreed in writing.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your property

    Send us the details through the host form: where the property is, what it is, and where you are with it now. It takes a few minutes and commits you to nothing.

    Everything you send stays private. Owner details are never published on this site.

  2. 02

    Property assessment

    We look at the property, the market and the local rules, then come back to you with what we would manage, what we would change first, and what we think the realistic constraints are.

    If we do not think short-term letting is right for the property, we will say so.

  3. 03

    We manage the operations

    Once the scope and fee are agreed in writing, we take over the agreed work: guests, turnovers, maintenance coordination and the listing itself.

    You set the spending thresholds we work within before anything starts.

  4. 04

    You stay informed

    You get regular written updates on what happened at the property, what it cost, and what we recommend next - plus a direct line to us whenever you want one.

    Owner dashboards are on the roadmap; until then, reporting is written and direct.

Questions

Common questions from property owners

Read all FAQs

What is Airbnb co-hosting?

Co-hosting means someone else runs the day-to-day operation of your short-term rental while you remain the owner and host of record. The listing stays in your account, you keep the final say on pricing and house rules, and the co-host takes on the work: guest messaging, check-in coordination, cleaning schedules, maintenance and the constant small decisions that come with letting a property.

Can you manage my existing Airbnb property?

Yes, and that is the most common way owners come to us. We can take over an established listing without resetting its review history or its ranking. We usually audit the listing first and agree the changes with you before making them.

Do you guarantee a certain income or occupancy?

No. Short-term rental performance depends on demand, seasonality, competition, your property and your pricing, and none of those can be guaranteed by a management company. Any firm promising guaranteed revenue is either not being straight with you or is hiding the conditions. We will tell you what we would change and report honestly on the results.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the property, the location and how much of the operation you want us to take on, so we quote after the property assessment rather than publishing one headline number. You get the fee and the scope in writing before anything starts.

Which Canadian locations do you serve?

Our current service areas are listed on the Service Areas page. We are deliberate about this: a market only works if we have reliable cleaning and maintenance capacity there, so we would rather tell you we do not cover somewhere than take on a property we cannot run properly. If your property is outside the listed areas, get in touch anyway and we will be straight with you.

Can guests book directly?

Not yet. Direct booking is on the roadmap and the site has been built for it, but we are not going to put a booking button on a page that cannot actually confirm a reservation. For now, enquiries come to us and we confirm availability with you. Where a property is also listed on Airbnb, that link is on the property page.
